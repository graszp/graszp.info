Definition

The comforter is the Name of God as the presence that consoles: the Paraclete, the advocate who stands beside; the shepherd of the twenty-third Psalm.

Is there a comforter? No, but there is comfort: empathy, companionship, and heterosubjectivity. The notion of God is heterosubjectivity applied to the ENTIRETY of reality, and the comforter is that notion at its most personal.

History Note

  • Psalm 23: the LORD as shepherd, who leads beside still waters.
  • Jesus promises another advocate, the Paraclete (John 14:16, 26).
  • Boethius, in prison, is consoled by Lady Philosophy (The Consolation of Philosophy, 524).
  • Feuerbach: God is the projection of human feeling and longing (The Essence of Christianity, 1841).

Scope Note

The comfort is real. The question is whether its source is a person beyond the world or the persons beside us, and what is lost when the one is mistaken for the other.
